# Nook wallpaper prompts

**How to use**
1. Open your AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot…).
2. Copy one whole prompt below (the scene text **and** the "Always add" part) and send it.
3. If the tool lets you choose a shape, pick **wide / landscape (16:9)**.
4. Download the image and drop it into this **Wallpapers-In** folder. File names don't matter.
5. Don't like it? Ask the tool "make another version" — keep the one you love.

Make as many as you like; 12 is a great first set. Tell Claude when some are in the folder.

---

## Always add this to the end of every prompt

> Wide 16:9 landscape desktop wallpaper. Highly detailed, painterly digital illustration with rich, cinematic, warm-and-cool lighting and a cozy winter mood. Gentle snowfall. Keep the bottom tenth of the image calm, open snowy ground with nothing important in it, and keep the very top strip as plain sky. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people.

## 1. Hearthlight
A snow-covered log cabin at night beside a frozen lake. Warm golden light glows from its windows and open door, colourful string lights hang along the thick snowy roof, and smoke curls from a stone chimney. Tall snow-laden pine trees frame the scene. Across the lake, a tiny alpine village twinkles under dark blue mountains, a crescent moon and a sky full of stars.

## 2. Lakeside Village
A quiet alpine village of wooden chalets along the shore of a frozen lake at night, every window glowing amber, a small church steeple in the middle. Snowy mountains rise behind under a deep blue starry sky; the village lights reflect softly on the ice. Snowy pine trees and a wooden fence in the foreground.

## 3. Rose Dusk
A wide snowy mountain valley at sunset with a glowing pink, peach and violet sky. Soft pastel light touches the snowy peaks. A small cabin with a warm window sits among snowy pines on a gentle hill, with a winding path of footprints leading to it.

## 4. Aurora Lake
Green and violet northern lights ripple across a starry sky above a frozen lake. A small wooden hut with a glowing lantern sits on the snowy shore beside dark, snow-dusted pine trees. The aurora reflects faintly on the ice.

## 5. Window Seat
Inside a cozy room at night, looking out through a large frosted window onto a snowy street with glowing lamp posts. On the wide window sill: a steaming mug of hot cocoa with marshmallows, a lit candle, a knitted blanket, a small potted pine, and warm fairy lights strung across the window frame.

## 6. Market Square
An old European town square on a snowy night, decorated for the holidays: wooden market stalls with warm lights and garlands, a tall Christmas tree covered in lights in the middle, old stone buildings with glowing windows, snow on the cobblestones, iron street lamps.

## 7. Lantern Path
A path winding through a quiet snowy pine forest at blue hour. Old iron street lamps along the path cast warm pools of golden light on the fresh snow. Snow-heavy branches, soft falling snow, a peaceful hush.

## 8. Bright Morning
A sparkling sunny winter morning: a snow-covered meadow with a wooden fence, a red barn with a snowy roof, frosted trees, and a clear pale blue sky. Fresh snow glitters in the low golden sunlight with long soft blue shadows.

## 9. Brownstone Evening
A city street of brick townhouses in the evening after a snowfall. Snow covers the front steps and railings, wreaths with red bows hang on the doors, windows glow warmly, and old street lamps light the snowy sidewalk.

## 10. Corner Café
A tiny café on a snowy street corner at night. Its big glowing shop window shows shelves of pastries and warm lights inside; string lights hang over the awning, a bicycle covered in snow leans against the wall, and snow piles softly on the window ledge.

## 11. Starlit Pines
A calm, minimal scene: silhouettes of snowy pine trees on a gentle hill beneath an enormous starry sky with the Milky Way. A small tent on the hill glows warm orange from a lantern inside.

## 12. Gingerbread Lane
A row of colourful, snow-covered cottages decorated for Christmas: wreaths on the doors, candy-cane coloured lights along the roofs, glowing windows, and a cheerful snowman with a scarf in a front garden. Early evening with a soft lavender sky.
